Herp Burst

Type an iNaturalist username to turn their reptile and amphibian life list into a collage of cutouts, with the species total in the middle.

Backgrounds are removed in your browser. The first run downloads a small model, and each photo takes a second or two, so a long list takes a few minutes.
Uses public observations from iNaturalist. Photos belong to the people who took them and carry their own licences, so check a photo's licence before sharing or selling a collage. Not affiliated with iNaturalist.
